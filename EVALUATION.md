## Framing

All evaluation results in this document are measured on **synthetic data**
generated by `packages/salvage-sim`. They measure the simulator's model of
reality, not reality itself. This distinction is stated here and must appear
on the first line of every results section.

The primary claim is **estimator validation against known synthetic ground
truth**: our off-policy estimators recover the true policy value to within a
reported margin on held-out simulated data where truth is known. This is a
statement about the evaluation methodology, not about production performance.
