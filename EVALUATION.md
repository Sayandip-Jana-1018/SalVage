## 7. Limitations & Empirical Boundaries

1. **Simulation vs Production Reality**: These evaluations measure the causal simulator's calibrated transitions. In production, unmodeled network partitions or cascading CBS outages could alter transition probabilities.
2. **Propensity Overlap Assumption**: Offline estimators require that all target actions have non-zero probability in the logging distribution ($p_i > 0$). Hard boundary-enforced actions (e.g. Quiet Hours) form deterministic strata where only the Direct Method is identifiable.
3. **Customer Friction Modeling**: Customer annoyance from repeated nudges is modeled with fixed economic penalties (150-200 paise); long-term unsubscription attrition requires online A/B validation.
