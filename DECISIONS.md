# Architecture Decision Records

Every significant technical decision is recorded as an ADR in
[`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/). Each record captures the context, the decision,
the consequences, and the alternatives considered.

ADRs are immutable once accepted. If a decision is superseded, a new ADR is
written that references the old one. The history of decisions is as important
as the current state.
